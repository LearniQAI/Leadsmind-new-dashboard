1. Purpose & Product Philosophy

LeadsMind must not become a generic bulk WhatsApp-number uploader. WhatsApp marketing must be a permission-based marketing system.

A CRM contact and a WhatsApp marketing recipient are different states.

CRM Contact
    |
    +-- WhatsApp Number
    +-- Consent Record
           |
           +-- ACTIVE?
           +-- Correct Business?
           +-- Not Revoked?
           +-- Not Suppressed?
           v
   WHATSAPP MARKETING ELIGIBLE

2. Non-Negotiable Anti-Spam Rules

  1. No CSV upload followed by immediate WhatsApp broadcasting.
  2. No purchased contact lists becoming marketing eligible.
  3. No scraped/harvested numbers becoming marketing eligible.
  4. No manually entered numbers becoming marketing eligible.
  5. No normal CRM imports becoming marketing eligible.
  6. No manual assignment of WHATSAPP_MARKETING_ELIGIBLE.
  7. No “Send Anyway” bypass.
  8. No removal of the mandatory consent filter.
  9. No cross-business use of consent.
  10. No ordinary-user override of opt-outs/suppression.
  11. No public API that simply sets eligibility to true.
  12. Every campaign must pass backend consent and suppression checks immediately before queueing.
  13. Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform/API, not consumer WhatsApp automation.

If a user attempts to add the protected status: “This is a protected system status. Contacts can only become eligible after completing an approved LeadsMind WhatsApp consent process.”

11. Trust & Safety / Customer Enforcement

Create a dedicated WhatsApp Trust & Safety layer covering consent validation, suppression, campaign validation, abuse detection, rate limiting, tenant monitoring, policy enforcement and audit logging.

Signals

  • High opt-out activity
  • High block/report activity
  • Repeated policy failures
  • Suspicious consent patterns
  • Attempts to bypass CSV restrictions
  • API manipulation attempts
  • Abnormally high form submissions
  • Repeated campaign violations

Internal states

NORMAL
MONITORED
RESTRICTED
SUSPENDED
TERMINATED

Use these as internal trust-and-safety states, not as a public “Meta compliance score.”

Do not promise Meta immunity. LeadsMind can implement safeguards, but Meta/WhatsApp retains independent enforcement authority.

14. Duplicate / Previous Opt-Out Handling

If an active consent exists, show that the number is already subscribed. If the person previously opted out, preserve the historical opt-out and do not let an ordinary user simply switch the contact back to eligible.

17. Security & Multi-Tenancy

Every WhatsApp resource must belong to a tenant. Tenant isolation must be enforced at the backend and data-access layers.

Tenant A → Contacts / Consent / Campaigns / WhatsApp Account
Tenant B → Contacts / Consent / Campaigns / WhatsApp Account

NO CROSS-TENANT ACCESS
  • Encrypt sensitive credentials at rest.
  • Never expose tokens to frontend clients.
  • Use HTTPS.
  • Verify webhook authenticity.
  • Apply RBAC and tenant authorization.
  • Rate-limit public forms and APIs.
  • Prevent replay/duplicate webhook processing.
  • Log security-sensitive actions.

No ordinary administrator button should exist for “Make Eligible.” Administrative intervention, if ever necessary, must use a controlled compliance workflow with an audit trail.

19. Meta / WhatsApp Alignment

The product should be built around the official WhatsApp Business Platform requirements. Meta's WhatsApp Business policy states that businesses may contact people on WhatsApp where the person has provided their mobile number and permission to receive subsequent messages from the business; the opt-in should identify the business and indicate that the person wants to receive messages. Businesses must respect requests to stop or opt out.

Business-initiated conversations generally require approved message templates on the WhatsApp Business Platform. LeadsMind should therefore synchronise template status and prevent campaigns from using unavailable templates.

Official policy: Meta / WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy

For South African customers, review the final consent and direct-marketing workflow against POPIA and applicable Information Regulator guidance before production.

20. Policy Monitoring

Meta may change messaging requirements, template rules, pricing, quality requirements, enforcement, API requirements and account restrictions. Keep policy-dependent behaviour configurable and review the official requirements before production releases.

26. Acceptance Criteria

Form

  • Business can create a WhatsApp Marketing Form.
  • WhatsApp icon is displayed.
  • Correct business identity is displayed.
  • Consent is unchecked by default.
  • Terms acceptance is required.
  • Privacy Policy is accessible.
  • Submission creates an auditable consent record.

Eligibility

  • Valid approved consent creates eligibility.
  • CSV imports do not create eligibility.
  • Manual entries do not create eligibility.
  • Normal CRM imports do not create eligibility.
  • Users cannot manually add the protected status.
  • Revoked consent removes eligibility.

Campaigns

  • Every campaign requires marketing eligibility.
  • The eligibility filter cannot be removed.
  • Ineligible and suppressed contacts are excluded.
  • Backend validation happens immediately before queueing.
  • No bypass mechanism exists.

Security

  • Tenant isolation is enforced.
  • API manipulation cannot create eligibility.
  • Users cannot override suppression.
  • Sensitive credentials are protected.
  • Consent and campaign actions are audited.
  • Suspended tenants cannot send marketing campaigns.

27. Final Developer Directive

Zain: Do not build WhatsApp Marketing as a normal broadcast feature with a consent checkbox added on top.

Build the Consent Engine first, then make the Campaign Engine depend on it.
CONSENT
   ↓
ELIGIBILITY
   ↓
SUPPRESSION
   ↓
AUDIENCE
   ↓
CAMPAIGN
   ↓

Final product principle: LeadsMind should make it technically difficult for customers to spam people. Businesses should build their WhatsApp audience through their own websites, landing pages, LeadsMind forms and approved consent flows, with an auditable record of how every marketing recipient became eligible.
