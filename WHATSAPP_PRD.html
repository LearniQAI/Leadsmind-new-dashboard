1. Executive Summary

LeadsMind will provide an integrated WhatsApp Broadcasting and Campaign Management system directly inside the LeadsMind CRM. Businesses will manage contacts, consent, audiences, approved WhatsApp message templates, personalised campaigns, scheduling, delivery status, conversations, reporting and CRM activities from one platform.

The product must be built around the official WhatsApp Business Platform/API rather than automating the normal WhatsApp mobile application. WhatsApp communication should become a native CRM capability connected to contacts, leads, customers, deals, appointments, invoices, payments, LMS events, support and automations.

2. Product Vision

Core workflow: Import/manage contacts → record consent → segment audience → create campaign → select approved template → personalise → preview → validate → schedule/send → process through queue → receive webhook statuses → report results → update CRM.

3. Objectives, Users & Scope

Primary Objectives

  • Connect an eligible WhatsApp Business number.
  • Manage WhatsApp contacts and consent.
  • Create segmented broadcast campaigns.
  • Use approved WhatsApp templates.
  • Personalise messages with CRM fields.
  • Schedule or send campaigns.
  • Track sent, delivered, read and failed states.
  • Handle inbound messages and opt-outs where supported.
  • Connect WhatsApp activity to CRM records.

Target Users

  • Business Owner: campaigns, contacts, reporting, permissions.
  • Marketing Manager: audiences, templates, campaigns, scheduling.
  • Salesperson: conversations, lead history and follow-up.
  • Support Agent: inbound conversations and customer history.
  • LeadsMind Administrator: integration, account and platform health.

Non-Goals

  • No automation of the consumer WhatsApp application.
  • No scraping of WhatsApp contacts.
  • No unofficial bulk-sending mechanisms.
  • No bypassing of WhatsApp template, account, messaging or enforcement requirements.

Idempotency

Every outbound message needs an internal unique ID and idempotency key. Retries, worker restarts and network failures must not create duplicate sends.

Retries

Retry temporary failures according to a controlled policy. Permanent failures must stop retrying and retain error information.

Pause / Cancel

Allow scheduled campaigns to be cancelled. For active campaigns, provide a “Stop Remaining Messages” action. Messages already submitted externally may not be reversible and must remain accurately recorded.

Webhook

POST /webhooks/whatsapp

Webhook processing must verify authenticity according to the integration requirements, identify the tenant/account/number, store the event, update the relevant message/conversation, trigger CRM automation where applicable, and respond correctly.

Webhook processing must be idempotent and should store an external event ID where available.

Conversation Window Logic

Distinguish between customer-initiated conversations, business-initiated messages, template messages and replies within the applicable customer-service window. The backend must determine whether the requested message type is permitted before sending.

10. Security, RBAC, Audit & Multi-Tenancy

Tenant Isolation

Every WhatsApp account, number, contact, campaign, template, message, conversation and event must belong to a tenant. Cross-tenant access must be impossible at the application and data-access layers.

Roles

RoleTypical Permissions
OwnerFull access
AdminWhatsApp configuration and campaigns
Marketing ManagerCampaigns, audiences, templates
SalespersonAssigned conversations and permitted messaging
Support AgentConversations and support actions
ViewerRead-only reporting

Security Requirements

  • Encrypt sensitive credentials at rest.
  • Never expose tokens to frontend clients.
  • Use HTTPS.
  • Verify webhook requests.
  • Apply tenant-level authorisation on every protected resource.
  • Apply role-based permissions.
  • Log security-sensitive actions.
  • Rotate credentials where supported.
  • Store only necessary sensitive information.

Audit Log

Record connection changes, campaign creation/editing/scheduling/cancellation, template changes, consent changes, exports and other security-sensitive actions with user, tenant, resource, timestamp and relevant before/after values.

14. Engineering Principles

  1. Official API first: use the WhatsApp Business Platform/API.
  2. Backend enforcement: consent, permissions and messaging rules must not rely only on frontend checks.
  3. Asynchronous messaging: campaigns use queues and workers.
  4. Idempotency: retries must not cause duplicate messages.
  5. Multi-tenancy: tenant isolation is mandatory.
  6. Auditability: important actions and message states must be traceable.
  7. Extensibility: design WhatsApp as a messaging infrastructure layer that can later support Email, SMS and other channels.
  8. Human approval: AI may prepare content and campaigns, but the user remains responsible for approving outbound campaigns.
Final Product Definition:
LeadsMind WhatsApp Broadcasting should become a complete WhatsApp CRM communication system allowing businesses to manage contacts, consent, campaigns, templates, conversations, automation, scheduling, reporting and customer communication from one platform.
